Generate only Grafcet source models.

Use an xmi:XMI root containing exactly one Grafcet:Grafcet element.

Declare:
- xmlns:xmi="http://www.omg.org/XMI"
- xmlns:xsi="http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema-instance"
- xmlns:Grafcet="Grafcet"

Represent contained nodes under Grafcet:Grafcet as:
- <elements xsi:type="Grafcet:Step" .../>
- <elements xsi:type="Grafcet:Transition" .../>
- <connections xsi:type="Grafcet:StepToTransition" .../>
- <connections xsi:type="Grafcet:TransitionToStep" .../>

Use positional references for connection endpoints:
- from="/0/@elements.N"
- to="/0/@elements.N"

Every StepToTransition must point from a Step to a Transition.
Every TransitionToStep must point from a Transition to a Step.

Do not explicitly serialize inverse references:
- grafcet
- incomingConnections
- outgoingConnections

Do not instantiate PetriNet target classes.
Do not generate target models.
